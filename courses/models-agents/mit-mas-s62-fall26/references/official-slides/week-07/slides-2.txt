Part II
• Main takeaway - to learn without forgetting, we should do on-policy learning.


• On-policy RL is limited to scalar rewards.


• But there are many types of learning signals:
   - Textual feedback
   - Hints
   - Detailed Instructions
   - And more
                     In-Context Learning
• The ability of Large Models to change their behavior based their context.

• Doesn’t have to be examples - works well with feedback, scalar rewards,
 hints and more.
